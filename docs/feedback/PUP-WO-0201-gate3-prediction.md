## My prediction, in the terms the gate asks about

| tile | what I predict a stranger says | passes? |
|---|---|---|
| 👋 | "a wave", "hello", "hi" — and *hello* is what the tile is | **likely PASSES** |
| 🌀 | "a swirl", "a spiral", "a hurricane", "weather", "loading" | **likely FAILS** |

**The specific claim I expect to be wrong about, or right about for the wrong reason:**
🌀 is a *weather* symbol before it is a *toy* symbol, and "loading" is the association a
screen teaches. A stranger may well name the picture correctly — "a spiral" — while being
unable to say **what tapping it does**, which is what the gate asks. Naming the drawing is
not naming the function.

**And the honest caveat about 👋:** it may pass for a reason that does not generalise —
`hello` is a placeholder, not a game, so "it says hello" is both the right answer and a
description of something with no content. A gate passed by the tile that does the least is
weak evidence about the tile that does the most.

**If 🌀 fails, that is a finding about the ICON, not about the picker**, and the fix is a
registry field — one entry, no picker edit, which is §2.1's contract doing its job.
